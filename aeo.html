Win the featured snippet, the People Also Ask box, and the voice search answer.

What's Included

The content structure answer boxes are pulled from.

Featured snippet targeting

Direct, 40 to 60 word answers placed right under a heading phrased as the exact question people search.

People Also Ask coverage

Content that answers the related questions Google shows alongside your target query, not just the main one.

Voice search optimization

Conversational, question-and-answer content structured for how people actually speak to Siri, Alexa, and Google Assistant.

List & table snippets

Snippet tracking

Monthly checks on which target queries you hold a featured snippet or PAA result for, and which ones are still open.

AEO vs. GEO

Same content discipline, two different targets.

AEO and GEO both reward answer-first, well-structured content built on a solid SEO foundation. They differ in where that answer shows up.

AEOGEO
Target surfaceFeatured snippets, PAA, voice search, in Google itselfAI-generated answers in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini
Ranking dependencyYou generally need to already rank on page oneRanking helps but citation depends more on structure and crawler access
How you measure itSnippet ownership for target queriesBrand mentions in AI answers to target questions

FAQ

AEO questions, answered.

AEO, or Answer Engine Optimization, is the practice of structuring content so search engines can lift a direct answer out of your page and show it as a featured snippet, a People Also Ask result, or a voice search answer.

AEO targets answer boxes inside traditional search engines like Google, for example featured snippets and voice search. GEO targets citations inside generative AI tools like ChatGPT and Perplexity. The content techniques overlap, but the target surface is different.
