Choosing an Approach

Native, cross-platform, or a Progressive Web App?

The right approach depends on budget, timeline, and how much the app needs to talk to the device itself.

ApproachBest forTrade-off
Native (iOS / Android)Deep device integration, top performance, camera or hardware-heavy appsHigher cost, two codebases to maintain
Cross-platformMost business apps: booking, loyalty, internal tools, marketplacesOne codebase, small performance trade-off vs. native
Progressive Web AppFast launch, no store approval needed, works everywhere with a browserLimited access to some native device features
